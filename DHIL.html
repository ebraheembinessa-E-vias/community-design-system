DHILKUWAIT

An architecture studio in Kuwait City

A house that keeps its shade. And opens to the air.

We draw courtyard houses for Kuwait's sun. Lime plaster that breathes, a wind tower that cools without electricity, and a teak screen that turns the street's glare into points of light. Twenty-seven houses since 2007. Three on site now.

01 · The courtyard

Every room opens onto the courtyard, not the street.

A 7 × 5.5 m court at the heart of the house, paved in turquoise zellige cut in Fez, with a shallow pool and two date palms. By day the walls throw shade across it. By night cool air settles into it, and the bedrooms above breathe it in.

7 × 5.5metres of open court
4.8°cooler than the street at 3 pm

02 · The wind tower

The tower catches the wind up high and brings it down cool.

A 4.2 m badgir on the north-west corner, its vents painted teal on all four faces. It takes the high air, passes it over a wet clay jar, and drops it into the majlis. On a July afternoon it moves 900 cubic metres of air an hour and lowers the room by six to eight degrees. No compressor, no bill.

6–8°cooler in the majlis

03 · The screen

The screen lets the light in and keeps the heat out.

A six-metre teak mashrabiya across the upper front: 156 turned pieces in a brass frame. It cuts the direct sun on the glass by seventy percent, keeps the family room private from the street, and lets the evening breeze through the whole floor.

04 · Materials

Six materials, chosen for forty summers.

Lime plasterthe walls — it breathes, and never cracks in the heat
Kuwaiti sea stonethe steps, the pool coping, the forecourt
Burmese teakthe screen, the door, the canopy over the court
Terracottathe parapet caps and the band around the court
Brassframes, studs, handles — it darkens with the years
Zellige turquoisethe courtyard floor, hand-cut in Fez

06 · The process

From your plot to your keys, in four stages.

  1. 1The briefWeeks 1–2. We walk your plot with you at 8, at 12 and at 4, and we listen more than we draw.
  2. 2The drawingsWeeks 3–8. Plans, a sun study for every room, and a 1:50 model that stays with you.
  3. 3The permitsWeeks 9–16. We file with the municipality and answer every note ourselves.
  4. 4The build14 to 18 months. We visit the site every Tuesday until you hold the keys.
Fees from 6% of the build cost, fixed at the drawings stage
